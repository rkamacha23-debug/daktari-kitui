Our Purpose Lengo Letu

Healing rooted in culture and tradition

Uponyaji uliojikita katika utamaduni na mapokeo

Many problems people face are not purely physical. They stem from cultural and spiritual imbalances — family disputes, ancestral matters, unexplained misfortune, or emotional burdens passed down through generations.

Matatizo mengi ambayo watu wanakabiliana nayo si ya kimwili tu. Yanatokana na usawa wa kitamaduni na kiroho — migogoro ya kifamilia, mambo ya mababu, bahati mbaya isiyoelezeka, au mizigo ya kihisia iliyopitishwa kwa vizazi.

Daktari Kitui uses time-honoured African remedies and traditional insight to address these culturally inflicted problems, offering a safe, respectful, and confidential space for healing.

Daktari Kitui hutumia dawa za Kiafrika zilizothibitishwa kwa muda mrefu na maarifa ya jadi kushughulikia matatizo haya yanayotokana na utamaduni, akitoa nafasi salama, ya heshima, na ya siri kwa uponyaji.
